# Top 25 AI/ML Engineer Hiring Platforms (India & Remote Focused)

This directory features the premier platforms, job boards, and specialized networks for sourcing or securing AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Generative AI roles. It is specifically tailored for the **Indian tech ecosystem** and **global remote opportunities**.

---

## 🎯 1. India-Centric Tech Heavyweights
*The most widely used job boards and professional platforms with the highest density of tech talent and active recruiters in India.*

* **LinkedIn**
  * **Link:** [https://www.linkedin.com](https://www.linkedin.com)
  * **Best For:** Global networking, outbound sourcing, and direct applications for local and remote AI engineering roles.
* **Naukri**
  * **Link:** [https://www.naukri.com](https://www.naukri.com)
  * **Best For:** High-volume sourcing across India. The undisputed market leader for resume databases.
* **Hirist**
  * **Link:** [https://www.hirist.tech](https://www.hirist.tech)
  * **Best For:** India's premium tech-only job board, featuring deep pipelines for Generative AI, MLOps, and Core Data Science.
* **Foundit (Formerly Monster APAC)**
  * **Link:** [https://www.foundit.in](https://www.foundit.in)
  * **Best For:** Broad corporate and startup tech hiring within India and Southeast Asia.
* **Instahyre**
  * **Link:** [https://www.instahyre.com](https://www.instahyre.com)
  * **Best For:** AI-driven matching that eliminates spam applications, connecting top-tier Indian tech candidates with top employers.
