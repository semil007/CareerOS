# AI/ML Engineer — Inforizon

Job ID: INFIT001

Hiring / Immediate joiners

Senior AI/ML Engineer

Locations: Ahmedabad

Experience Required: 5+ Years
Position: Permanent role

Send Resume: recruiter@inforizonhr.com/7306892755

ABOUT THE ROLE
You will build and improve the models we run ourselves. You will fine-tune, evaluate, and
optimize open-source and custom models, and work closely with our applied and platform
engineers to turn them into reliable production systems.
Our stack: Python and Node.js (TypeScript) services across Azure and Google Cloud, a React
front end, and a mix of hosted AI APIs (Azure OpenAI, Vertex AI) and self-hosted open-source
models on our own GPUs.
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
– Fine-tune and evaluate open-source ML / deep-learning and large language models for our
specific use cases in construction-related software development.
– Build and maintain data pipelines, training loops, and evaluation harnesses.
– Run experiments, track results, and iterate on model architecture, data, and
hyperparameters.
– Optimize models for accuracy, latency, and cost, and prepare them to be served on our
GPUs.
– Partner with our applied and platform engineers to deploy and serve models reliably at
scale.
REQUIREMENTS
Must have
– Experience: Mid-level or above – typically 3+ years in ML/AI, including hands-on model
training, fine-tuning, or evaluation.
– Strong foundation in machine learning and deep learning fundamentals.
– Hands-on experience with Python and frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
– Proven experience fine-tuning and evaluating open-source models (LLMs or transformers)
for real use cases.
– Experience preparing models for production, serving and optimizing them on GPUs (e.g.
quantization, batching).
– Solid understanding of data preparation, experiment design, and evaluation metrics.
– Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate across engineering, product and
design.
– Comfortable working in a fast-moving environment with evolving requirements.
– A bias toward writing clean, tested, maintainable code (or reproducible analysis).
Nice to have
– Experience with distributed training or advanced optimization (distillation, LoRA / PEFT).
– Familiarity with model-serving frameworks (vLLM, Triton) and GPU infrastructure on Azure or Google Cloud.
– Publications, competition results, or open-source contributions in ML.
Educational qualifications
– Computer Science degree in Artificial Intelligence from a reputable university or an
equivalent experience.
