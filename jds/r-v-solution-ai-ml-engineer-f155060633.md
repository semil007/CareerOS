# Job Title: AI/ML Engineer

## \*\*About the Role\*\*

We are looking for an experienced \*\*AI/ML Engineer\*\* to design, develop, optimize, and deploy cutting-edge machine learning and generative AI solutions. In this role, you will bridge the gap between AI research and production systems by building robust data pipelines, fine-tuning large language models (LLMs), and driving MLOps practices.

If you are passionate about pushing the boundaries of AI, working with modern GenAI tech stacks, and building scalable systems, we’d love to have you on our team in Jaipur.

## \*\*Key Responsibilities\*\*

\* \*\*Model Development & Deployment:\*\* Design, train, evaluate, and deploy machine learning, deep learning, and Generative AI solutions across NLP, Computer Vision, and Predictive Analytics.

\* \*\*GenAI & RAG Solutions:\*\* Fine-tune, optimize, and deploy foundation models/LLMs. Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures using vector databases.

\* \*\*Data & Feature Engineering:\*\* Build scalable data processing pipelines, feature engineering workflows, and handle both structured and unstructured datasets efficiently.

\* \*\*MLOps & Automation:\*\* Establish CI/CD pipelines for machine learning, model monitoring, automated retraining, version control, and governance in production.

\* \*\*API & Microservices Integration:\*\* Expose ML models via robust REST/gRPC APIs and microservices for seamless product integration.

\* \*\*Performance Tuning & Experimentation:\*\* Benchmark models, optimize latency and resource utilization, and ensure security and reliability at scale.

\* \*\*Cross-Functional Collaboration:\*\* Partner with product managers, data engineers, and business stakeholders to translate domain requirements into actionable AI solutions.

## \*\*Required Skills & Qualifications\*\*

### \*\*Core Technical Skills\*\*

\* \*\*Programming:\*\* Advanced proficiency in \*\*Python\*\*.

\* \*\*ML Frameworks:\*\* Strong hands-on experience with \*\*PyTorch\*\*, \*\*TensorFlow\*\*, and \*\*Scikit-learn\*\*.

\* \*\*Foundational Knowledge:\*\* Solid grasp of core ML algorithms, deep learning architectures, NLP, and statistical modeling.

\* \*\*Databases:\*\* Hands-on experience with relational (SQL) and non-relational (NoSQL) databases.

### \*\*AI / LLM Stack\*\*

\* Experience with orchestration frameworks: \*\*LangChain\*\* or \*\*LlamaIndex\*\*.

\* Familiarity with the \*\*Hugging Face\*\* ecosystem and \*\*OpenAI APIs\*\*.

\* Practical knowledge of \*\*Vector Databases\*\* (e.g., Pinecone, Milvus, Qdrant, ChromaDB, PGVector).

\* Hands-on expertise in \*\*Prompt Engineering\*\*, \*\*PEFT/LoRA fine-tuning\*\*, and model serving frameworks (e.g., vLLM, TGI, Triton).

### \*\*MLOps & Cloud Infrastructure\*\*

\* \*\*Cloud Platforms:\*\* Experience deploying on \*\*AWS\*\*, \*\*Azure\*\*, or \*\*GCP\*\*.

\* \*\*Containerization & Orchestration:\*\* Solid understanding of \*\*Docker\*\* and \*\*Kubernetes\*\*.

\* \*\*MLOps Tools:\*\* Knowledge of tools like MLflow, Kubeflow, DVC, or similar CI/CD pipelines for machine learning.
