# Data Annotation & AI Evaluation Freelancer — RubricCore AI

RubricCore AI is an AI data services company. We help AI companies review model responses, AI agents, conversations, and training data through careful human evaluation and quality checks. We are building a network of skilled freelancers to work with us on current and upcoming projects.

We're looking for detail-oriented people to join our freelance network for data annotation, transcription, and AI response evaluation work. * Annotate or label data according to project requirements
* Flag errors or inconsistencies during quality checks
* Follow structured task guidelines and meet agreed timelines

* Strong attention to detail
* Good written English and ability to follow instructions precisely
* Comfortable working independently and meeting deadlines
* Reliable internet access and a computer
* Prior experience in data annotation, transcription, or content review is a plus, not required
