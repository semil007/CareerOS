# Assistant Manager - MIS — Magic Bus India Foundation

*Assistant Manager MIS*
*Job Description*

Reporting To

Senior Manager - M&E

Deployment

Project Office

Job Title

Assistant Manager MIS

Required Qualifications

· Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) or Bachelor’s degree in any technical field

· 6-7 years of prior work experience in education, technology programs, MIS and survey management

· Proficiency in English (written & spoken) and Telugu for school communication and reporting

· Strong skills in data compilation, dashboard creation and reporting

· Basic computer literacy in Excel, Power BI, AI tools and other data analysis software

· Experience in government partnership programmes is desirable

Key Responsibilities

· Create an all-inclusive strategy for the operation of the project Management Information Systems (MIS).

· Lead the development and rollout of session and activity tracking technology platforms.

· Compile and manage project data including master data, session delivery, attendance and number of sessions conducted.

· Generate weekly, fortnightly and monthly dashboards for project monitoring and tracking.

· Review field data regularly and provide feedback to project and impact teams.

· Perform routine data quality checks to ensure accuracy and relevance of reported information.

· Report concurrent monitoring data to regional offices and head office, including government and funder reporting requirements.

· Coordinate with all project locations to ensure timely data updates.

· Support Program Managers and Impact Managers in project reviews and monitoring activities.

· Conduct field visits to verify data reporting processes and ensure data quality compliance.

· Provide periodic training to project staff on MIS systems and M&E processes.

· Collaborate with central and regional teams to review project data health and provide recommendations.

· Submit reports and participate in district and state-level review meetings as required.

Pay: ₹600,000.00 - ₹750,000.00 per year

Benefits:
* Cell phone reimbursement
* Provident Fund

Work Location: In person
