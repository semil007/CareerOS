Job Purpose

We are looking for a Junior Conversational AI / Chatbot Engineer with around 3 years of experience to join the Omnichannel team. The primary focus of this role is to strengthen the chatbot and automation capabilities within the Sprinklr platform and help build scalable self-service journeys that reduce dependency on human support.The candidate will work on chatbot flows, intent classification, automated responses, case deflection, knowledge base integration, handover-to-agent logic and backend integrations with CRM, SAP and other enterprise systems. The role requires someone who understands conversational design and automation, but also has enough technical capability to support APIs, data flows, testing and omnichannel platform configuration.Purpose of the RoleThe main objective of this role is to help build chatbot-led customer service automation across channels such as WhatsApp, web/self-service, email and voice-to-digital journeys. The candidate should help convert repeatable customer queries into automated journeys, while ensuring smooth escalation to human agents when required.Key Responsibilities

Key Accountabilities

As a Junior Conversational AI / Chatbot Engineer, you will: Design, build and maintain chatbot flows for customer service use cases.Configure intents, utterances, entities, decision trees, conversation flows and fallback paths.Build automation journeys for common service requests such as order status, delivery status, invoice queries, case status, product information and complaint intake.Work on Sprinklr chatbot, AI agent and conversational automation capabilities.Support AI classification and routing logic so incoming customer messages are understood and directed correctly.Integrate chatbot journeys with CRM, SAP, case management, knowledge base and other backend systems through APIs.Build smooth handover logic from chatbot to human agent, including context transfer, case creation and routing.Support knowledge base integration so the chatbot can provide consistent and approved responses.Monitor chatbot performance, containment rate, fallback rate, escalation rate and customer satisfaction.Identify high-volume support topics that can be automated to reduce manual workload.Test chatbot journeys across channels such as WhatsApp, webchat and other digital touchpoints.Support UAT, defect resolution, production validation and post-go-live hypercare.Maintain chatbot documentation, flow diagrams, test scripts, release notes and configuration records.Work closely with product owners, business analysts, customer service teams, Sprinklr consultants and integration teams.
