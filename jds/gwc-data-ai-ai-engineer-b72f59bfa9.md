# AI Engineer — GWC Data.AI

*AI Engineer*

*Experience:* 2+ Years
*Employment Type:* Full-Time
*Location:* Hosur, Tamil Nadu (Work from Office)

*Job Summary*

We are looking for an experienced AI Engineer with 2+ years of hands-on expertise in building AI-powered applications using Machine Learning, Generative AI, and Agentic AI. The ideal candidate should be passionate about developing intelligent solutions using Large Language Models (LLMs), AI agents, and modern AI frameworks to solve real-world business problems.

*Key Responsibilities*

* Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML and Generative AI applications.
* Build and implement *Agentic AI* solutions using AI agent frameworks.
* Develop intelligent applications using *LLMs*, *RAG*, and Prompt Engineering.
* Fine-tune and integrate LLMs for enterprise use cases.
* Build scalable APIs and deploy AI models in production environments.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver AI-driven business solutions.
* Optimize AI models for performance, accuracy, and scalability.

*Required Skills*

* Strong proficiency in *Python*.
* Hands-on experience with *Machine Learning* and *Deep Learning*.
* Experience in *Generative AI (GenAI)* and *Large Language Models (LLMs)*.
* Experience building *Agentic AI* applications using *LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen*, or similar frameworks.
* Strong knowledge of *Prompt Engineering* and *Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)*.
* Experience with *LangChain*, *LlamaIndex*, and AI orchestration frameworks.
* Hands-on experience with *OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Hugging Face*, or similar platforms.
* Knowledge of *TensorFlow*, *PyTorch*, or *Scikit-learn*.
* Experience with *Vector Databases* (FAISS, Pinecone, ChromaDB, Milvus).
* Experience in developing REST APIs using *FastAPI* or *Flask*.
* Familiarity with *SQL*, *Git*, *Docker*, and cloud platforms (*AWS, Azure, or GCP*).
