# Graphic Designer & Video Editor — Websoftrix Infotech

We are looking for a creative and highly skilled Designer/Video Editor to join our growing digital media team. The ideal candidate should have strong experience creating engaging Instagram Reels, TikToks, YouTube Shorts, and other short-form content, with the ability to turn professionally shot footage into high-quality, attention-grabbing videos.

Experience with AI-powered creative tools for generating video clips, AI voiceovers, visual effects, image/video generation, and other content workflows is highly preferred. A creative mindset and willingness to explore new AI technologies will be a strong advantage.

You should have a strong eye for transitions, pacing, storytelling, music, sound effects, captions, visual effects and current social media trends.

*Key Responsibilities*

* Edit engaging Instagram Reels, TikTok videos and YouTube Shorts
* Edit real estate videos, property tours, luxury home videos and promotional content
* Edit makeup, beauty, fashion and lifestyle videos
* Create creative transition-based videos
* Add professional transitions, effects, animations and motion graphics
* Perform colour correction and colour grading
* Add music, sound effects, captions, subtitles and text animations
* Create strong hooks and engaging pacing for short-form content
* Use AI-powered editing tools to improve workflow, creativity and efficiency
* Incorporate VFX and visual effects when required
* Reformat and optimize videos for different social media platforms
* Keep up with current Instagram and TikTok trends, editing styles and viral formats
* Work with provided footage and creative direction while also bringing your own creative ideas

*Required Skills*

Strong experience with three or more of the following:

* Adobe Premiere Pro
* Adobe After Effects
* Adobe Photoshop
* DaVinci Resolve
* CapCut Pro
* Adobe Illustrator

*We are looking for someone who:*

* Has a strong creative eye
* Understands what makes short-form content engaging
* Has excellent knowledge of social media video editing
* Can create smooth and professional transitions
* Understands timing, pacing, music and storytelling
* Has experience editing real estate, fashion, beauty, makeup or lifestyle content
* Is comfortable using AI tools for video editing and content creation
* Has knowledge of VFX, motion graphics and visual effects
* Can work independently and meet deadlines
* Is detail-oriented and takes pride in producing high-quality work
* Is constantly learning and following the latest social media trends

*What We Provide*

* High-end professional video footage/ Client detailed requirements
* Creative direction and content concepts
* A variety of projects across real estate, fashion, beauty, lifestyle and business
* Opportunity to work on content designed for high-growth social media platforms
* Flexible working environment
* Opportunity for ongoing work and growth within the company
