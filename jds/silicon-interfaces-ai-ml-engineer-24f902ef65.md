About Silicon Interfaces

Silicon Interfaces is a leading Software and VLSI Design Center focused on developing cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Agentic AI solutions. Our AI/ML division specializes in building intelligent AI models, domain-specific language models, autonomous AI agents, and enterprise-grade AI workflows for industries including Semiconductor, Education, Human Resources, and Sales.

We are expanding our AI & Machine Learning team and are looking for passionate, innovative, and highly motivated Data Science Engineers who are eager to work on next-generation AI technologies.

---

Job Summary

As a Data Science Engineer, you will be responsible for designing, developing, and deploying scalable Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions. You will contribute to the development of Small Language Models (SLMs), Large Language Models (LLMs), Agentic AI workflows, Shared Memory APIs, Vector Databases, and intelligent AI agents for industry and educational applications.

You will work alongside experienced engineers to build reliable, scalable, and production-ready AI systems while continuously learning emerging technologies.

Key Responsibilities

\* Design, develop, and optimize AI/ML models for real-world applications.

\* Develop and fine-tune Small Language Models (SLMs) and Large Language Models (LLMs).

\* Build and integrate Agentic AI workflows and intelligent autonomous AI Agents.

\* Develop Shared Memory APIs and Vector Database integrations.

\* Work on AI applications for Semiconductor, Education, Sales, and HR domains.

\* Design scalable, maintainable, and well-tested software solutions.

\* Perform data preprocessing, model evaluation, and performance optimization.

\* Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams throughout the software development lifecycle.

\* Continuously explore and implement emerging AI technologies and best practices.
