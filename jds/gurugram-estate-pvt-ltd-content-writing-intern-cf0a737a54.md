# Content Writing Intern — Gurugram estate pvt ltd.

Content Writer Intern

*Company:* TrueAsset Consultancy Pvt. Ltd.
*Location:* Gurugram, Haryana

About the Role

We are looking for a creative and motivated *Content Writer Intern* to join our team. The ideal candidate should have a strong command of written English, a creative mindset, and an interest in creating engaging content for social media, websites, blogs, and marketing campaigns.

Key Responsibilities

* Write engaging content for *Instagram, LinkedIn, websites, blogs, and marketing campaigns*.
* Create captions, social media posts, hooks, scripts, and promotional content.
* Research industry trends, topics, and competitors to develop relevant content.
* Assist in developing *content calendars and campaign ideas*.
* Write and edit content while maintaining proper grammar, clarity, and brand tone.
* Coordinate with the marketing and creative team for content requirements.
* Optimize website/blog content for basic *SEO practices*.
* Proofread and edit existing content for accuracy and readability.
* Stay updated with social media trends and emerging content formats.

Requirements

* Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in *English, Journalism, Mass Communication, Marketing, or a related field*.
* Excellent written English and grammar.
* Strong research and communication skills.
* Creative thinking and ability to generate fresh content ideas.
* Basic understanding of social media platforms.
* Knowledge of *SEO, Canva, or AI content tools* is an advantage.
* Freshers and candidates with relevant writing samples are welcome.

What You'll Learn

* Professional content writing and copywriting
* Social media content strategy
* SEO and blog writing
* Brand communication
* Content planning and marketing
* Working with real-world marketing campaigns

Who Should Apply?

If you enjoy *writing, researching, creating ideas, and understanding what makes people stop scrolling*, this internship is for you.

*To Apply:* Send your CV along with your writing samples/portfolio.
