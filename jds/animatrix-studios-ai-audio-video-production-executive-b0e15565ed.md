# AI Audio-Video Production Executive — Animatrix Studios

Animatrix is looking for a creative and technically skilled *AI Audio-Video Production Executive* to join our production team.

The candidate will work on AI-assisted video production, voice generation, image generation, editing, content creation and other audio-visual projects for YouTube, digital platforms, branded content and professional productions.

Key Responsibilities

* Create AI-generated videos, images and visual sequences
* Generate and process AI voice-overs
* Work with AI video generation and image-to-video tools
* Create prompts for AI image, video and audio generation
* Edit and assemble AI-generated footage into finished videos
* Work on voice, music, sound effects and basic audio processing
* Create short-form and long-form digital/video content
* Work closely with the creative and production teams
* Experiment with new AI tools and workflows to improve production quality and speed
* Maintain consistency of characters, visuals and style across AI-generated sequences

Preferred Skills

Candidates with hands-on knowledge of some of the following will be preferred:

* ChatGPT / Gemini / Claude or similar AI tools
* Midjourney / Flux / Stable Diffusion or other AI image tools
* Runway / Kling / Veo / Hailuo / Pika or similar AI video tools
* ElevenLabs or other AI voice-generation tools
* Basic audio editing / sound design
* Prompt Engineering

Candidates do *not* need to know every tool listed above. Strong creative sense, willingness to learn and practical knowledge of AI-based content production are more important.

Pay: ₹12,000.00 - ₹20,000.00 per month

Benefits:
* Provident Fund

Work Location: In person
