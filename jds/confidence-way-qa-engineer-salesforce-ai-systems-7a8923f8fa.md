# QA Engineer – Salesforce & AI Systems — Confidence Way

We are looking for a QA Engineer to help us maintain the quality and reliability of our Salesforce environment as well as our AI-powered products, including chatbots and agent AI systems.

*Key Responsibilities*

* Design and execute test cases for Salesforce configurations, customizations, and third-party integrations (ERP SAP..)
* Evaluate AI chatbots and agent AI systems for accuracy, consistency, and appropriate handling of edge cases and failures
* Assess multi-step, multi-tool AI workflows to confirm agents behave as intended across an entire task, not just a single response
* Conduct functional, regression, integration, and user acceptance testing across web and API layers
* Identify, document, troubleshoot, finding root cause and track defects, working closely with the development team through to resolution
* Maintain clear and thorough test documentation for both conventional features and AI-driven functionality
* Support automation testing initiatives where relevant, or help establish them if they do not yet exist
* Work with product and engineering teams to translate requirements into meaningful test coverage

*Requirements*

* 2–5 years of professional experience in quality assurance or software testing
* Practical experience testing Salesforce, including workflows, custom objects, or integrations
* Experience testing AI chatbots, conversational AI, or agent AI systems
* Solid grounding in QA fundamentals, including test case design and defect lifecycle management
* Ability to test systems where outcomes are not always fixed or fully predictable
* Strong written communication skills, particularly when explaining AI behavior to non-technical stakeholders
* Ability to work independently and reliably in a remote setting

*Preferred Qualifications*

* Experience with automation testing tools such as Selenium, Playwright, or Cypress
* Familiarity with API testing tools such as Postman
* Exposure to prompt evaluation or LLM output assessment methods
* Relevant Salesforce certifications

Work Location: Remote
