# AI Engineer — Hexa Coder Technologies

*Company:* Hexa Coder Technologies
*Location:* Ahmedabad (On-site)
*Experience:* 2–3 Years
*Employment Type:* Full-Time

About Hexa Coder Technologies

Hexa Coder Technologies is an innovative technology company specializing in 3D applications, Digital Twins, Product Configurators, Real-Time Rendering, AI-driven solutions, Computer Vision, Generative AI, and modern web technologies.

We work on challenging and technology-driven projects that combine AI, 3D visualization, automation, and software engineering to build meaningful solutions for real-world applications.

Job Overview

We are looking for a skilled and self-driven *AI Engineer* with *2–3 years of hands-on experience* to join our Ahmedabad team. The ideal candidate will have practical experience building and deploying AI/ML solutions, working with Generative AI and LLM-based applications, and integrating AI models into production-grade software. This role requires someone who can work independently, take ownership of AI features end-to-end, and contribute to technically challenging projects involving AI, 3D visualization, and automation.

Key Responsibilities

* Design, develop, and deploy AI/ML models and solutions for real-world business use cases.
* Build and integrate Generative AI and LLM-based applications (e.g., RAG pipelines, prompt engineering, fine-tuning, agentic workflows).
* Own the AI/ML lifecycle — data collection, preprocessing, feature engineering, model training, evaluation, and deployment.
* Integrate AI models and APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, open-source LLMs, etc.) into production applications.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams (software engineers, 3D/graphics developers, product teams) to implement AI-powered features.
* Optimize model performance, latency, and accuracy for production environments.
* Work on Computer Vision and NLP tasks as needed, depending on project requirements.
* Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code following best practices.
* Debug, test, and troubleshoot AI models and pipelines in development and production.
* Stay current with emerging AI/ML and Generative AI technologies and evaluate their applicability to ongoing projects.
* Mentor junior team members/interns on AI/ML fundamentals and best practices, as needed.

Required Skills

* 2–3 years of professional experience in AI/ML engineering or a related role.
* Strong proficiency in *Python* and common ML/DL libraries (e.g., NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, PyTorch or TensorFlow).
* Practical experience with *Generative AI / LLMs* — prompt engineering, RAG, fine-tuning, or building LLM-powered applications.
* Solid understanding of core ML concepts: supervised/unsupervised learning, classification, regression, model evaluation, and optimization.
* Experience integrating AI models via REST APIs into software applications.
* Familiarity with version control (Git) and standard software development practices.
* Experience working with cloud platforms (AWS/Azure/GCP) or ML deployment tools is a plus.
* Strong problem-solving, debugging, and analytical skills.
* Good communication and teamwork skills; ability to work independently in an on-site, collaborative environment.

Good to Have

* Experience with Computer Vision (OpenCV, YOLO, image/video processing).
* Exposure to NLP techniques and libraries (spaCy, Hugging Face Transformers).
* Experience with vector databases (Pinecone, Chroma, FAISS, Weaviate) for RAG applications.
* Familiarity with MLOps practices — model versioning, CI/CD for ML, containerization (Docker).
* Experience with 3D applications, Digital Twins, or real-time rendering technologies.
* Prior contributions to AI/ML projects, open-source work, or published research.
* Knowledge of web technologies (JavaScript/React, Node.js) for building end-to-end AI-powered applications.
