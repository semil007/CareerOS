. Research and evaluation of emerging technology, industry and market trends to assist in project development and/or operational support actives
. Transforms business requirements into technical architecture requirements, and designs application architectures which are scalable, robust, and manageable, while balancing the price/performance Provides technical guidance or business process expertise, technical leadership, coaching and mentoring to team members

. Enforces proper integration and link of different applications within the Enterprise.
. Evaluates alternative architectural approaches.

. Ability to interact with Business stakeholders and solve business problems spanning across functions
