# Senior AI Automation Engineer — EVERMORE GLOBAL IFSC PVT LTD.

We are looking for a visionary and hands-on *Senior AI Automation Engineer* to architect, scale, and lead the development of our next-generation AI-powered automation infrastructure. In this role, you will design complex multi-agent systems, advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, and robust backend architectures to power autonomous enterprise workflows. *Experience:* 3+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong, focused track record in backend engineering, AI integration, and workflow automation.

You will serve as the technical lead for our automation stack, turning high-level business challenges into scalable, production-grade systems. We value deep engineering rigor, architectural foresight, and a proven track record of deploying autonomous agents to production.

Responsibilities

* *Architect AI Agents & Workflows:* Design, build, and scale autonomous multi-agent systems and complex workflows utilizing frameworks like LangChain, LangGraph, and custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers.
* *Scale Backend & API Infrastructure:* Build and maintain high-performance backend services, asynchronous workers, and robust REST/GraphQL APIs using Python and FastAPI.
* *Advanced RAG & LLM Integration:* Architect enterprise-grade RAG systems, embedding pipelines, and semantic search layers, seamlessly integrating frontier LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini).
* *Robust Automation & Scraping:* Oversee complex browser automation and web-scraping pipelines using Playwright alongside workflow tools like n8n.
* *Deployment & DevOps:* Manage end-to-end cloud deployments, containerization, and infrastructure scaling across environments using Docker, Railway, Vercel, and PostgreSQL.
* *Technical Leadership:* Mentor junior engineers, establish coding standards, conduct thorough code reviews, and drive architectural decisions from conception to production.

Requirements & Qualifications

* *Experience:* 5+ years of software engineering experience, with at least 3+ years specifically focused on backend engineering, AI integration, and workflow automation.
* *Core Tech Stack:* Expert-level proficiency in *Python*, *FastAPI*, *PostgreSQL*, *Git/GitHub*, and deployment platforms (*Railway*, *Vercel*).
* *AI & Agent Frameworks:* Deep hands-on experience with LLM orchestration frameworks (*LangChain*, *LangGraph*), vector databases, and agent architectures (*MCP*, tool-calling).
* *Automation & Scraping:* Proven expertise in browser automation tools (*Playwright*) and workflow orchestration platforms (*n8n*).
* *Infrastructure:* Strong command of *Docker*, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud-native application deployment.
* *Builder Mentality:* A portfolio of production-grade systems, open-source contributions, or complex AI projects that demonstrate a history of solving real-world business bottlenecks.
