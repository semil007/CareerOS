# AI/ML Engineering Manager — Thomson Reuters

Reuters Technology builds and runs the systems behind one of the world's largest and most trusted news organisations. Among them is a specialist machine-learning platform that screens and classifies media content at scale. In production since 2019, running every day, and now moving quickly from classical machine-learning models toward large-language-model and generative-AI pipelines on AWS.

First and foremost, we're looking for an outstanding AI/ML engineer to take technical ownership of this platform. You'll spend most of your time in the models and the code - designing, building, evaluating and shipping - and you'll set the technical direction for where the platform goes next.

It's also a role with scope to lead. You'll be the anchor for a small team and you'll be the technical point of contact for the platform's stakeholders, including a US-based partner Thomson Reuters business. We'd equally welcome a senior or staff engineer ready to grow into that and an engineering lead who has stayed genuinely close to the code.

,,About the Role :

Build and own the platform (hands-on) — the core of the role

Take end-to-end technical ownership of our media-screening platform, both its classical ML models and its newer LLM/GenAI pipelines, running in production on AWS.
Spend most of your time hands-on: design, build, evaluate and ship models and the services around them.
Drive the migration from classical ML toward LLM and generative-AI approaches, deciding pragmatically where modern techniques earn their place and where proven models should stay.
Own reliability, cost, quality and MLOps discipline for a system that runs in production every day.

Set direction and lead

Set the technical direction, standards and roadmap for the platform, and make the calls on architecture and approach.
Be accountable for delivery — planning, prioritisation, and keeping a daily-production system healthy.

Represent and partner

Be the technical point of contact for the platform's stakeholders, including a US-based partner Thomson Reuters business, build trust and manage expectations.
Partner with product, editorial and other technology teams to surface and shape high-value use cases.

Explore and expand

Identify and prototype new applications of ML/AI across Reuters, turning a single-purpose platform into reusable capability.

About You:

This role is likely a strong match if you recognise yourself in most of the points below. Above all we want a genuinely strong AI/ML engineer.

Substantial, currently hands-on experience building and shipping ML/AI systems in production: you have written the code and trained and evaluated the models yourself and still do. (Typically gained over 5+ years in ML / softML/softwareware engineering.)
Recent, hands-on AWS experience (e.g. SageMaker, Lambda, S3, ECS/EKS) and infrastructure-as-code.
Strong Python, and a solid grounding in both classical ML and modern LLM/GenAI techniques (fine-tuning, prompting, evaluation, retrieval).
Sound MLOps instincts: reproducible training, deployment, monitoring and cost and quality management.
The judgement to know when to reach for an LLM and when a simpler model wins.
Confidence working with stakeholders across geographies and organisational boundaries, including representing the platform to a US-based partner business.
Excellent communication: able to explain complex technical trade-offs to technical and non-technical audiences alike.
Experience with content, media, NLP or large-scale classification problems.
A track record of modernising or migrating legacy ML systems to LLM/GenAI.
Familiarity with Terraform and AWS CDK.
Experience building or hiring a team, or managing stakeholders across time zones.
