The Team

The Service Enablement Platform (SEP) team is responsible for multiple critical platforms that enable the servicing across all channels at American Express. This includes Case Platforms that are used for Global Disputes Management, Fraud Servicing, Credit, Collections and Back-Office Case processing besides Global Card Issuance and Automation.

From creating next-generation applications and microservices to leveraging AI, you could be doing transformational work that brings our iconic, global brand into the future. American Express is making continuous investments for building and scaling these platforms with security, resilience and innovation to meet our growth agenda and maintain trust, loyalty and premium experience for our customers. We are looking for strong leaders and engineers to be part of high energy and efficient teams that will bring our mission to life.
We don’t hire to a narrow checklist, but candidates should be comfortable operating in a modern, enterprise-scale engineering environment with a strong emphasis on agentic AI.
