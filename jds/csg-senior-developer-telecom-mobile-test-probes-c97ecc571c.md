# Senior Developer - Telecom Mobile Test Probes — CSG

Are you passionate about software development for mobile network integration? Join our team to help take our telecom testing technology to new heights.

The CSG Assure team is developing an advanced telecom testing software solution used by mobile operators across the globe, including leading operators like AT&T, Tata, Orange.

CSG Assure is an artificial test traffic generation platform consisting of a cloud-based application, controllers and test probes located in around 200 countries globally.

We are looking for a skilled developer to be part of our small, agile team to build applications that help our mobile operator customers validate and optimize mobile service performance for their roamers and local subscribers.

You will develop our next-generation mobile test probe for 4G, 5G and IoT to simulate mobile users using voice, SMS and data services. Main development is done in C & C++ on Linux platform.

We think that you are a highly skilled, analytical, customer-focused person with a strong developer profile that enjoys taking responsibility, making sure our customers get full value of our high-tech solutions.

Required Skills

Completed Degree in Computer Science or related field

5+ years of professional experience in software development, preferably in telecom

Strong proficiency in C++, data structures and multi-threading programming

Linux system knowledge

Solid understanding of the Radio Access Network (RAN) layer of mobile networks; the signaling and data exchange between devices and the network.

Good knowledge of mobile network technologies (3G, 4G, IMS/VoLTE, 5G, IoT) and network signaling (e.g. SIP, RRC, NAS) as defined in 3GPP

Talent for AI driven development

Excellent Communication, problem-solving skills and ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.

Bonus Skills

Experience in development against cellular modems; experience with either AT commands, QMI, or modem vendor SDK

Experience with network monitoring tools and protocol analyzers like Wireshark, QCAT

Familiarity with scripting languages, e.g. LUA, Shell, Perl

Understanding of eUICC/eSIM, GSMA standards

Android development experience

Why should you apply?

Opportunity to work and grow your skillset in cutting-edge technologies
