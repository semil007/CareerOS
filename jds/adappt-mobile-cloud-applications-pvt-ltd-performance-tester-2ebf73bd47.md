*Job Description – QA Performance Tester *

*Role:* QA Performance Tester
*Location:* Remote
*Availability:* Immediate

*About the Role*
We are looking for an experienced *QA Performance Tester* to join the Create+ team on an immediate basis. The ideal candidate will have strong experience in performance testing of web applications and be comfortable working in a modern technology environment involving *C#, React and AWS*.

*Key Responsibilities*

- Design and execute performance, load, stress, and scalability testing.
- Develop and maintain performance test scripts and test scenarios.
- Analyse performance results, identify bottlenecks, and provide actionable recommendations.
- Work closely with developers, QA engineers, and other stakeholders to troubleshoot performance issues.
- Validate application performance across different environments and workloads.
- Prepare performance test reports and communicate findings to technical and business stakeholders.
- Support performance testing as part of the overall QA and release process.

*Required Skills & Experience*

- Proven experience in *QA Performance Testing*.
- Hands-on experience with performance testing tools such as *JMeter, LoadRunner, k6, Gatling or similar*.
- Experience testing *web applications*.
- Good understanding of API, database and application performance testing.
- Experience working with *C#/.NET, React and AWS* environments.
- Strong understanding of performance metrics, bottleneck analysis and root-cause investigation.
- Ability to work independently and collaborate effectively with distributed teams.

*Nice to Have*

- Experience testing *Microsoft Word Add-ins*, particularly Office/Word integrations.
- Experience with cloud-based performance testing and AWS services.
- Experience working in Agile development environments.
- Automation/scripting experience.
