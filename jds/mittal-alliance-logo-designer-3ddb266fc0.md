# Logo Designer — Mittal Alliance

Logo Designer (Adobe Illustrator Expert)

We are looking for a creative and skilled Logo Designer to join our team. The ideal candidate should have strong experience in designing professional, unique, and brand-focused logos using Adobe Illustrator and other Adobe Creative Suite tools.

Responsibilities

* Design original and high-quality logos for clients across various industries.
* Create multiple logo concepts and revisions based on client feedback.
* Develop brand identity elements, including color palettes and typography recommendations.
* Deliver vector files and other required formats for print and digital use.
* Ensure all designs are professional, scalable, and aligned with brand objectives.

Requirements

* Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator and other Adobe Creative Suite applications.
* Strong portfolio showcasing logo and branding projects.
* Ability to create custom logo designs with minimal reliance on AI-generated artwork.
* Good understanding of typography, color theory, and brand identity design.
* Attention to detail and ability to meet project deadlines.
* Strong communication and collaboration skills.

Preferred Qualifications

* Experience working with startups, agencies, or corporate brands.
* Knowledge of branding and visual identity systems.
* Ability to provide creative concepts and strategic design recommendations.

If you are passionate about creating unique and impactful brand identities through professional design work, we would love to hear from you.

Pay: ₹2,000.00 - ₹8,000.00 per week

Work Location: Remote
