# UI/UX Designer — ZoftSolutions Pvt Ltd

We are looking for a creative and user-focused *UX/UI Designer* to design intuitive experiences for AI-powered applications and enterprise SaaS platforms.

*Key Responsibilities:*

* Collaborate with product managers, AI engineers, and solution architects to design user-centric interfaces.
* Create user flows, wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes.
* Conduct user research and usability testing to improve product experience.
* Build and maintain design systems, reusable components, and style guides.
* Design dashboards and visualizations for AI agents, workflows, and system performance.
* Work closely with frontend developers to ensure accurate implementation of designs.
* Promote user-centered design while balancing business and technical requirements.

*Requirements:*

* *3-6 years of experience in UX/UI design*.
* Strong portfolio showcasing SaaS, enterprise, AI/ML products, or data-rich dashboards.
* Hands-on experience with Figma, Sketch, or Adobe XD.
* Good understanding of HCI, accessibility, and responsive design principles.
* Experience designing cloud-based or enterprise applications (AWS is a plus).
* Strong communication and collaboration skills.

*Good to Have:*

* Exposure to AWS services such as Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker, Lambda, or Step Functions.
* Experience with conversational interfaces, AI dashboards, or agentic AI UX.
* Knowledge of D3.js, Chart.js, or similar data visualization frameworks.
* Experience working in Agile/Lean UX environments.
