# STEEL Fabrication Engineer — Pooja Enterprises

*Job Title:* STEEL Fabrication Engineer – Structural Steel Fabrication
*Location:* HOWRAH Kolkata, India]
*Industry:* Steel Fabrication / Construction / Infrastructure
*Experience Required:* Minimum 10 Years
*Reporting To:* Head – Projects / Operations Manager
*Employment Type:* Full-time

*Job Summary:*

We are seeking a *highly skilled and experienced Fabrication Engineer* with strong expertise in *structural steel fabrication*, both *on-site and in-shop*, to join our growing operations. The ideal candidate will have *10+ years of hands-on experience* in structural fabrication projects, solid command over *CAD design/drafting*, and a deep understanding of industrial fabrication techniques. A blend of *engineering knowledge and practical field expertise* is essential.

We will appreciate people with past experience from similar structural fabrication companies

*Key Responsibilities:Design & Technical Planning*

* Interpret structural drawings, blueprints, and client specifications to plan fabrication works.
* Prepare and modify fabrication drawings using *AutoCAD, SolidWorks*, or similar CAD tools.
* Coordinate with design and project engineering teams to ensure structural feasibility and safety.
* Optimize design for ease of fabrication and site erection, keeping material wastage and cost in check.

*Fabrication Oversight*

* Lead and supervise fabrication activities at the *workshop and site*, ensuring compliance with design, quality, and safety standards.
* Develop cutting lists, fabrication schedules, welding plans, and assembly drawings.
* Coordinate material requirements and plan procurement in advance to avoid delays.
* Supervise *fit-up, welding, grinding, assembly*, and finishing of structures.
* Hands-on ability to guide workers in *manual and semi-automatic fabrication methods*.

*Project Execution & Site Management*

* Manage site fabrication and erection of structures, including planning for manpower, tools, and logistics.
* Resolve on-site fabrication issues in real time, coordinating between engineers, foremen, and subcontractors.
* Conduct regular site inspections and quality checks to ensure conformance with structural tolerances and safety protocols.
* Liaise with civil, electrical, and architectural teams for integrated project delivery.

*Team Management & Training*

* Lead a team of fabricators, fitters, welders, riggers, and helpers.
* Train junior engineers and supervisors in fabrication techniques and standards.
* Enforce compliance with safety practices and site discipline.

*Quality Control & Documentation*

* Ensure dimensional accuracy, welding quality, and structural integrity through stringent inspection.
* Maintain records of daily progress, welding logs, material traceability, and inspection reports.
* Prepare and review method statements, ITPs (Inspection & Test Plans), and material submittals.

*Key Skills & Competencies:*

* *Technical Skills:* Proficient in AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Tekla (optional), and other CAD software.
* Strong knowledge of structural steel fabrication, welding processes (MIG, TIG, SMAW), and surface finishing.
* Excellent reading of fabrication & erection drawings.
* Ability to work with hands and guide teams practically on-site and in the workshop.
* Knowledge of applicable codes such as IS, AWS, and BS standards for fabrication and welding.
* Strong project planning, time management, and problem-solving skills.

*Qualifications:*

* *Education:* Diploma or Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering / Fabrication Technology / Structural Engineering or related fields.
* *Experience:* Minimum 10 years in structural steel fabrication with at least 5 years in a supervisory or lead role at site.
* *Certifications:* Welding inspector certification (e.g., CSWIP or equivalent) is a plus.

*Desirable Traits:*

* Physically fit and capable of inspecting work on-site and at height.
* Proactive, disciplined, and safety-conscious.
* Team player with leadership ability and strong communication skills.
* Willingness to travel to project sites as needed.

*Compensation & Benefits:*

* Competitive salary based on experience and skills.
* Performance incentives and project completion bonuses.
* Accommodation and travel allowance for site-based work (if applicable).
* Opportunities for growth into Project Management or Design Lead roles.
