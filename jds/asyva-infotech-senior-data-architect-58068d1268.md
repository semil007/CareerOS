# Senior Data Architect — ASYVA INFOTECH

We are looking for a highly experienced *Senior Data Architect* to define, design, and drive enterprise data architecture across data warehousing, cloud migration, integration, analytics, governance, and modern data-platform initiatives.

The selected candidate will be responsible for developing enterprise data-architecture strategies, defining reusable standards and patterns, reviewing technical designs, and guiding engineering teams in building scalable, secure, governed, and high-performing data platforms.

Job Details

*Role:* Senior Data Architect
*Experience Required:* 10+ years
*Employment Type:* Contract or Full-Time, depending on client engagement
*Work Mode:* Remote after completion of initial onsite onboarding
*Initial Onsite Requirement:* Up to one week at the client office
*Notice Period:* Immediate to 15 days preferred

Key Responsibilities

* Define and maintain the enterprise data-architecture strategy, principles, standards, reference architectures, and technology roadmaps.
* Design scalable architectures for enterprise data warehouses, data lakes, data marts, lakehouses, operational data stores, and analytical platforms.
* Develop conceptual, logical, and physical data models aligned with business capabilities and enterprise standards.
* Define dimensional, relational, normalized, denormalized, and analytical data-modelling patterns.
* Apply Kimball, Inmon, Data Vault, and hybrid data-warehousing methodologies based on business and technical requirements.
* Design batch, near-real-time, streaming, event-driven, API-based, and file-based data-integration architectures.
* Define ETL and ELT architecture patterns for enterprise data pipelines and transformation frameworks.
* Review data models, schemas, ETL/ELT pipelines, APIs, integration designs, and cloud data-platform implementations.
* Establish frameworks for data governance, metadata management, data lineage, master data, reference data, and data quality.
* Define data-security, privacy, access-control, encryption, retention, masking, and compliance requirements.
* Support legacy-to-cloud migration, data-platform modernization, and application-decommissioning programmes.
* Evaluate and recommend appropriate cloud services, databases, integration platforms, modelling tools, and data-governance technologies.
* Define performance, scalability, reliability, availability, disaster-recovery, and cost-optimization strategies.
* Conduct architecture assessments, solution-design reviews, technical-risk reviews, and architecture-governance sessions.
* Prepare architecture documents, data-flow diagrams, solution blueprints, integration patterns, technical standards, and migration roadmaps.
* Work closely with data engineers, data modellers, analytics teams, application teams, security teams, and business stakeholders.
* Mentor technical teams and ensure adherence to architecture principles, engineering standards, governance requirements, and industry best practices.
* Present architecture recommendations, design decisions, risks, trade-offs, and implementation roadmaps to senior leadership and client stakeholders.

Mandatory Skills and Experience

* 10+ years of overall experience in data engineering, data architecture, data warehousing, data integration, or related areas.
* Strong hands-on experience defining enterprise data architectures and architecture roadmaps.
* Deep understanding of data warehouses, data lakes, data marts, lakehouses, and modern cloud data platforms.
* Strong experience in conceptual, logical, and physical data modelling.
* Expertise in Kimball, Inmon, Data Vault, or hybrid data-warehousing methodologies.
* Strong knowledge of dimensional modelling, relational modelling, star schemas, snowflake schemas, and analytical data models.
* Experience designing batch, real-time, streaming, and event-driven integration solutions.
* Strong understanding of ETL, ELT, CDC, API integration, messaging, orchestration, and enterprise data-pipeline architecture.
* Experience implementing data-governance, metadata, lineage, data-quality, and stewardship frameworks.
* Strong knowledge of data security, privacy, regulatory compliance, role-based access control, encryption, and data masking.
* Advanced SQL skills and strong understanding of database design, indexing, partitioning, performance tuning, and workload optimization.
* Experience with cloud migration, legacy-system modernization, and enterprise data-platform transformation.
* Ability to evaluate architectural trade-offs related to scalability, performance, reliability, security, maintainability, and cost.
* Strong stakeholder-management, communication, documentation, and architectural decision-making skills.
* Experience leading design reviews and guiding data engineers, data modellers, and technical delivery teams.
