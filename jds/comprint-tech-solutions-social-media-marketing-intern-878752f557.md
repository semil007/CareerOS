# Social Media Marketing Intern — Comprint Tech Solutions

Role
· Assist in planning and executing social media content across Instagram, LinkedIn and other relevant platforms.
· Research trending topics, AI developments, industry news and content formats to identify opportunities.
· Create and coordinate reels, carousels, static posts, stories and short-form videos. Write engaging captions, hooks, post copy and basic scripts.
· Assist with content calendars and ensure timely publishing.
· Monitor social media performance including reach, views, engagement, followers and content performance.
· Track trends, competitors and relevant creators in the AI and technology space.
· Work with the design and content team to turn ideas into social-first creatives.
· Support campaigns, events, webinars and other marketing initiatives.
· Help repurpose long-form content, talks and presentations into social media content.
· Stay updated on AI tools, emerging technologies and digital marketing trends.
· Support basic reporting and share insights on what content is working and what can be improved.

Who We’re Looking For
· Students or recent graduates from Marketing, Mass Communication, Media, Business, Journalism or a related field.
· Strong interest in social media, AI, technology and digital marketing. Good written and verbal communication skills.
· Comfortable with Instagram and LinkedIn and understands what makes content engaging.
· Creative mindset with the ability to come up with fresh content ideas. Basic understanding of Canva, Instagram, LinkedIn and video editing tools is preferred.
· Willingness to learn AI tools and experiment with new content formats. Proactive, organised and comfortable working in a fast-paced environment.

Good to Have
· Experience managing a college, personal or brand social media page.
· Basic knowledge of Canva, CapCut, Premiere Pro or similar tools.
· Familiarity with ChatGPT or other AI tools for content creation and research.
· Understanding of social media analytics and content performance.
· A portfolio of social media work, college projects, reels or personal content.

What You’ll Learn
· How a technology/AI brand builds its digital presence.
· End-to-end social media planning and execution. Content strategy and audience building.
· AI-powered content creation and marketing workflows. Social media analytics and performance optimisation.
· Brand communication and campaign execution. Working with marketing, creative and leadership teams.

Why Join AI Next?
This is a hands-on internship for someone who wants to build things, not just schedule posts. You will get exposure to AI, technology, content strategy, social media growth and real-world marketing campaigns while working closely with the team.
If you are curious about AI, love social media and constantly think, “this could be a reel,” we’d love to meet you.

Work Location: In person
