# AI/ML Engineer — iMark Infotech Pvt Ltd

*Job Title: AI/ML Engineer*

*Company: iMark Infotech*

*Experience Required: 5+ Years*

*Job Summary*
iMark Infotech is seeking a skilled and proactive AI/ML Engineer with 5+ years of hands-on experience in designing, developing, and deploying Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning solutions. The ideal candidate should possess strong technical expertise in AI/ML technologies, excellent communication skills, and the confidence to interact directly with international clients, gather requirements, present technical solutions, and successfully clear client interviews.

*Key Responsibilities*

\* Design, develop, test, and deploy AI/ML models and intelligent applications.

\* Build and optimize machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI solutions.

\* Develop scalable AI-powered applications using Python and related frameworks.

\* Work with large datasets, perform data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model evaluation.

\* Integrate AI/ML solutions with APIs, web applications, and cloud platforms.

\* Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI, Machine Learning, LLMs, and Generative AI technologies.

\* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and deliver innovative solutions.

\* Troubleshoot, debug, and optimize AI models and production systems.

\* Participate in code reviews and contribute to engineering best practices.

\* Communicate directly with clients to understand business requirements and propose technical solutions.

\* Represent the company in client meetings, technical discussions, and interviews with confidence and professionalism.

\* Successfully handle and clear client technical interviews while demonstrating strong communication and problem-solving abilities.

*Required Technical Skills*

\* Strong proficiency in Python.

\* Hands-on experience with AI/ML frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Keras, or similar.

\* Experience with Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI Agents, LangChain, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, or related technologies.

\* Good understanding of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, and model optimization techniques.

\* Experience in building and consuming RESTful APIs.

\* Familiarity with vector databases such as Pinecone, Weaviate, ChromaDB, or FAISS.

\* Experience with databases such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or similar.

\* Knowledge of cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.

\* Experience with Git and modern software development practices.

*Soft Skills*

\* Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

\* Strong client-facing and stakeholder management abilities.

\* Ability to confidently handle client interviews and technical discussions.

\* Strong presentation and requirement-gathering skills.

\* Excellent analytical and problem-solving capabilities.

\* Self-motivated, proactive, and quick learner.

\* Ability to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team.

*Preferred Qualifications*

\* Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments.

\* Exposure to MLOps, CI/CD pipelines, Docker, Kubernetes, and model deployment.

\* Experience with international clients and remote collaboration.

\* Contributions to AI/ML projects, open-source initiatives, or research publications are a plus.
