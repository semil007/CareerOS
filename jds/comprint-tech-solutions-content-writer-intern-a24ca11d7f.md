# Content Writer Intern — Comprint Tech Solutions

AI Next Studio is looking for a Content Writer Intern to create engaging, well-researched content for our House of Brands and one of our original IPs.
This role is ideal for someone who enjoys writing, researching and keeping up with the rapidly evolving worlds of technology, AI and innovation. You should be able to take complex or technical information and turn it into content that is clear, engaging and easy to understand.

What You'll Do
· Create content for brands under AI Next Studio's House of Brands and one of our original IPs.
· Research topics, trends, industries and developments relevant to the brands you work on.
· Write scripts for YouTube videos and Instagram Reels.
· Write copy for social media creatives, carousels and other digital content.
· Research and simplify technical topics into engaging, easy-to-understand content.
· Develop strong hooks, narratives and scripts suited to different platforms and audiences.
· Stay updated on the latest developments in AI, technology, innovation and emerging trends.
· Work closely with the creative, design and video teams to bring content ideas to life.
· Adapt your writing style based on different brands, audiences and content formats.
· Ensure content is accurate, well-researched and aligned with the brand's tone and voice.

What We're Looking For
· Strong writing, research and communication skills.
· A genuine interest in technology, AI, innovation and emerging trends.
· Someone who actively keeps up with the latest developments in the tech and AI space.
· Ability to research and understand complex or technical topics.
· Ability to turn technical information into simple, engaging and easy-to-understand content.
· Knowledge of servers, desktops, hardware, enterprise technology or related technology topics is a plus.
· A good understanding of storytelling, hooks and what makes content engaging.
· Familiarity with content formats across YouTube, Instagram and other digital platforms.
· Ability to adapt writing styles for different brands and audiences.
· Someone who is curious, creative, detail-oriented and open to feedback.
· Previous writing samples, scripts or a portfolio are preferred.

Work Location: In person
