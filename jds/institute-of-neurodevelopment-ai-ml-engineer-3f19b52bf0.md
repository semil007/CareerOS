*About IND – Institute of NeuroDevelopment*

The Institute of NeuroDevelopment (IND) is a mission-driven organisation committed to transforming the developmental journey of children with autism and other neurodevelopmental differences. Through evidence-based interventions, parent empowerment, structured progress tracking, and innovative educational systems, IND strives to unlock every child's potential.

As Artificial Intelligence becomes an integral part of healthcare, education, research, and organisational productivity, IND is building an AI-enabled ecosystem that supports clinicians, therapists, educators, researchers, operational teams, and families through intelligent software solutions.

We are looking for an AI/ML Engineer who enjoys solving real-world problems through Artificial Intelligence and building practical solutions that can be deployed across multiple organisational functions.

*Role Summary*

The AI/ML Engineer will design, develop, and deploy AI-powered applications that improve organisational productivity, automate workflows, enhance decision-making, and support the development of intelligent tools for both internal teams and client-facing services.

The role combines software engineering, AI integration, workflow automation, and applied machine learning to create scalable solutions that support IND's long-term vision.
