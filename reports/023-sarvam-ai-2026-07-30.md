**Junior-to-Mid** (2 years). The CV shows strong technical depth but limited breadth in infrastructure tooling (K8s, monitoring stack, CI/CD). However, the candidate has shipped production systems and contributed to open-source vLLM — that carries weight.

### "Sell Senior Without Lying" Plan

1. **Lead with production depth, not years.** Emphasise that every month of your 2 years was spent shipping production LLM systems — not intern projects or proof-of-concepts. Use the phrase "2 years of production experience compressed into a full-stack ML lifecycle."
2. **Highlight vLLM contributions.** Open-source contributions to a core inference engine show you operate at a level beyond your title.
3. **Frame founder/startup agility.** The candidate's previous roles (AK Technologies) involved end-to-end ownership — building from zero to production. This aligns with Sarvam's "high ownership from day one" culture.
4. **Quantify impact.** Use metrics: "40% throughput improvement via TensorRT-LLM", "35% latency reduction via Redis caching", "1,000+ requests/hour document processing". These speak to a senior engineer's outcomes.

### "If They Downlevel Me" Plan

- **Accept if compensation is fair** for the lower title (e.g., ML Ops Engineer L2 instead of L3). The opportunity to work on defence/strategic AI at a funded startup is valuable.
- **Negotiate a 6-month review** with clear criteria: get a promotion to the target level after demonstrating the missing K8s/CI/CD skills.
- **Ask for the promotion criteria upfront** — what does a "Senior ML Ops Engineer" look like at Sarvam? Then build a plan to hit those within 6 months.
