## C) Level and Strategy

### Level Detected
- **Title:** Lead Engineer AI/ML (Staff / Principal-equivalent IC)
- **JD Signals:** "Leads design and development", "Provides targeted feedback and peer review", "Collaborates with model builders and recommends architectures"
- **Experience band:** 8+ years → typically L6/L7 equivalent at large tech, Staff Engineer
- **Candidate's natural level:** L4 / Senior IC (2 years professional, but strong outputs)

### The Experience Gap

8+ years vs ~2 years is a 4x difference. This is not a "stretch" scenario — it's a level mismatch. Most large enterprises (Unisys included) have hard gates on years of experience for job banding.

### "Sell Senior Without Lying" Plan

Given the gap size, the honest strategy is **not to position for this exact level**, but to:
1. **Apply for the role** in case the "8+ years" is a soft requirement (many JDs inflate it)
2. **Alternative: target the Senior AI/ML Engineer level** at Unisys if it exists (likely 5-8 years)
3. If you get a phone screen, frame your experience as **compressed high-output**:
   - "I've delivered 2 production multi-agent systems, 2 document intelligence platforms, and fine-tuned a 70B model — all in 2 years. That's a density of outcomes that typically takes 4-5 years."
   - "I can show you a deployed multi-agent ERP assistant, a production TensorRT-LLM pipeline, and a multilingual document system processing 1,000+ docs/hour. These are real deployments, not POCs."
   - "What I lack in tenure I more than make up in execution speed and depth across the full stack — from fine-tuning to deployment to monitoring."

### "If They Downlevel Me" Plan

This is the most likely scenario if they're interested:
1. **Target Senior AI/ML Engineer** (expect 5-8 years) or **AI/ML Engineer II** (3-5 years)
2. **Negotiate:** Accept the downlevel if comp is fair for the level. Ask for a 6-month review with clear criteria for promotion
3. **Comp floor:** ₹25-35 LPA for Senior Engineer in Bangalore (based on training data for 2025-26)
4. **Promotion criteria to negotiate:** "If I deliver X (a production agentic system in the first 6 months), I want an expedited promotion path to Lead Engineer"

| 3 | RAG pipelines + vector databases | **Document intelligence for geospatial transport** | Urban transport planning team needed structured extraction from 1,000+ docs/hour across multiple Indian languages | Heterogeneous document formats, high accuracy requirements, multilingual | Built RAG pipeline with Gemini 1.5 for extraction + translation, vector store for retrieval, W&B for monitoring | Scaled to production, processing 1,000+ high-resolution requests/hour with full observability stack | Reflection: Document quality varied wildly — the RAG pipeline needed aggressive pre-processing normalization. | 6 | Kubernetes / containers / microservices | **Distributed GPU infrastructure** | Single-node inference was becoming bottleneck for multi-tenant usage | Needed to distribute across GPU nodes without adding latency | Containerized with Docker, used Redis for distributed state, designed health checks and fallback mechanisms | 35% latency reduction on multi-node inference, zero-downtime deployments | Reflection: I underestimated the complexity of GPU node health checks.
