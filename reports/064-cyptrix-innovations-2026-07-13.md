## C) Level and Strategy

### Level Mismatch

- **JD expects:** Senior (5+ years)  
- **Candidate’s natural level:** Junior-Mid (~2 years)  

### "Sell senior without lying" Plan

1. **Lead with production impact** – not years. Example: "Architected and deployed a multi-agent ERP assistant in production; fine-tuned Llama 3 70B and improved inference throughput 40%." That is senior-level delivery.  
2. **Show system ownership** – "Led RAG pipeline and document intelligence modules — end-to-end, from prototype to scaled production" signals seniority even with fewer years.  
3. **Quantify breadth** – "Processed 1,000+ high-resolution requests/hour with full observability" proves scale experience.  
4. **Highlight open-source contributions** – vLLM and LLMCache contributions differentiate you as a practitioner who contributes to the community, not just uses tools.  
5. **Use technical depth** – TensorRT-LLM, INT8/FP8 quantization, KV-cache optimization – these are advanced skills typically held by senior engineers.  

**Concrete phrasing for cover letter / interview opening:**  
> "While I have two years of professional experience, I've spent those two years shipping production AI systems end-to-end — from fine-tuning a 70B model to deploying multi-agent architectures with real-time inference optimization. My work has delivered measurable throughput gains and scaled to serve thousands of requests per hour."

### "If they downlevel me" Plan

- **Accept if comp is fair** — ₹80-95k/month for a mid-level (2-3 year) role in India is above market average.  
- **Negotiate a 6-month review with clear promotion criteria** — ask for milestones (e.g., number of production deployments, performance benchmarks).  
- **Title flexibility:** Mid-level AI/ML Engineer is still a good stepping stone for the next role. Do not accept below "Senior" if the salary doesn't compensate.
