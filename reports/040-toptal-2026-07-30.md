The experience gap (2 vs 5+ years) and absent frontend skills are structural. Suggestions if you still want to try:

- **If you're open to it:** Accept the assessment, demonstrate your AI depth, and negotiate a 6-month review for promotion to senior.
- **Risk:** Toptal's model is to match clients with verified talent. If you're not at the senior level, clients may not pick you.
