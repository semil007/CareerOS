### Level detected vs candidate's natural level

- **JD level:** Mid-Senior (6-8 years), likely IC3 or senior engineer in Bosch's band.
- **Candidate's natural level:** Junior-Mid (2 years professional experience). Appropriate for IC1/IC2 roles.

### "Sell senior without lying" plan

Not advisable — the gap is too large. Attempting to oversell would damage credibility. Instead:
- Frame the 2 years as "high-velocity, high-impact" — but be honest about the timeline.
- Highlight that the candidate has independently architected end-to-end systems (multi-agent assistant, document intelligence) that typical 6-year engineers haven't built.
- Quantify impact (40% throughput improvement, 35% latency reduction, 1000+ requests/hour) — these are outputs, not inputs (time invested).
- Emphasize breadth across the full stack (fine-tuning → quantization → deployment → monitoring).

### "If they downlevel me" plan

Downleveling would be likely (to IC1/IC2). Accept if:
- Compensation is fair for the role band at Bosch (₹12-18 LPA for IC1, ₹18-25 for IC2 — see Block D).
- Negotiate a 6-month review pathway to IC3 with clear criteria (e.g., ship one edge deployment, publish one blog post or internal paper).
- Do NOT accept without written commitment on growth milestones.
