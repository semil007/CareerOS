**Assessment:** The JD's "Staff" title is misleading. With 3-5 YOE required, this is closer to a **Senior Engineer** role in market-standard leveling. The candidate is still 1-2 years short of even that bar, but the production scope of their work (multi-agent systems, inference optimization at scale, multilingual document intelligence) is genuinely senior-caliber for their YOE.

### "Sell Senior Without Lying" Plan

1. **Lead with production scope, not tenure.** "Architected and deployed production-ready multi-agent ERP assistant" and "Built scalable multilingual document intelligence system processing 1,000+ requests/hour" demonstrate Staff-level impact regardless of YOE.
2. **Frame inference optimization as systems leadership.** TensorRT-LLM, vLLM, INT8/FP8 quantization, KV-cache optimization, distributed GPU with Redis — this is infrastructure-level work that goes beyond typical 2-YOE output.
3. **Position founder-adjacent mindset.** At AK Technologies, the candidate led modules "end-to-end, from prototype to scaled production deployment" — this mirrors the ownership the JD asks for.
4. **Mentoring angle:** Even without formal reports, frame technical leadership as "set architectural direction for RAG pipeline modules" and "designed health checks and fallback mechanisms for production reliability" — these are mentoring-adjacent activities (setting standards others follow).

### "If They Downlevel Me" Plan

- If offered Senior SWE (AI) instead of Staff: **Accept if comp is fair.** The title matters less than the work scope and growth trajectory at a company with Druva's market position.
- Negotiate a **6-month review** with clear promotion criteria to Staff.
- Use the MLOps + inference optimization work as leverage — these are high-value, scarce skills.
